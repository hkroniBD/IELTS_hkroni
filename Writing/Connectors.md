# 🌟 **Complete IELTS Band 9 Connector List (Master Edition)**

Use these to create variety, coherence, and precision in your essays. They’re grouped by function for easy memorization.

---

## 🔹 **1. Adding or Extending Ideas**

| Basic                | Advanced Equivalents                        |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| And                  | Moreover, Furthermore, In addition          |
| As well as           | Along with, Together with, Coupled with     |
| Not only... but also | Besides that, In addition to that, Moreover |
| Also                 | Additionally, Besides, What’s more          |
| Especially           | Particularly, Notably, Specifically         |
| Most importantly     | Above all, Chiefly, Primarily               |

📝 *Example:*

> The government should invest in education **as well as** healthcare to ensure national progress.

---

## 🔹 **2. Giving Examples or Illustrations**

| Basic       | Advanced Equivalents                 |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------ |
| For example | For instance, Namely, To illustrate  |
| Like        | Similarly, Likewise, Correspondingly |
| That’s why  | Hence, Thus, Accordingly             |

📝 *Example:*

> **For example**, renewable energy sources such as solar power can reduce dependency on fossil fuels.

---

## 🔹 **3. Showing Contrast or Opposition**

| Basic       | Advanced Equivalents                           |
| ----------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| But         | However, Nevertheless, On the contrary         |
| While       | Whereas, Meanwhile, At the same time           |
| In contrast | Conversely, On the other hand, Differently     |
| Despite     | Regardless of, In spite of, Albeit             |
| Even though | Although, Albeit, Though                       |
| Instead of  | Rather than, In place of, As an alternative to |

📝 *Example:*

> **Although** online learning offers flexibility, it lacks face-to-face interaction.

---

## 🔹 **4. Showing Cause and Effect**

| Basic           | Advanced Equivalents                        |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| So              | Therefore, Consequently, As a result        |
| Because         | Since, Owing to, Due to                     |
| For this reason | Due to this, Owing to this, On this account |

📝 *Example:*

> **Due to** the rising cost of living, many families are cutting back on non-essential spending.

---

## 🔹 **5. Sequencing and Ordering Ideas**

| Basic            | Advanced Equivalents                       |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| First / At first | Initially, To begin with, In the beginning |
| Next             | Subsequently, Thereafter, In succession    |
| After            | Later, Following, Afterward                |
| During           | While, Throughout, In the course of        |
| Before           | Prior to, Earlier, Previously              |
| Then             | Afterward, Following that, Thereafter      |
| At last          | Finally, Ultimately, In the end            |
| As soon as       | The moment, Instantly, Immediately         |
| When             | Whenever, At the time, As soon as          |

📝 *Example:*

> **Initially**, the company struggled, but it **eventually** achieved remarkable success.

---

## 🔹 **6. Emphasizing or Clarifying a Point**

| Basic      | Advanced Equivalents                   |
| ---------- | -------------------------------------- |
| To clarify | In other words, That is to say, Namely |
| Really     | Indeed, Undoubtedly, Significantly     |
| Very       | Extremely, Exceptionally, Remarkably   |
| No doubt   | Undoubtedly, Without a doubt, Surely   |

📝 *Example:*

> **Indeed**, technological innovation has revolutionized every aspect of daily life.

---

## 🔹 **7. Showing Comparison or Similarity**

| Basic     | Advanced Equivalents                      |
| --------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| Like      | Similarly, Likewise, Correspondingly      |
| While     | Whereas, Meanwhile                        |
| Similarly | In the same way, Equally, Correspondingly |

📝 *Example:*

> **Similarly**, teachers play a vital role in shaping students’ character and future.

---

## 🔹 **8. Expressing Purpose**

| Connector       | Example                                                            |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| In order to     | In order to reduce pollution, stricter laws are required.          |
| So as to        | The company invested in R&D so as to remain competitive.           |
| With the aim of | The plan was designed with the aim of improving healthcare access. |

---

## 🔹 **9. Expressing Condition**

| Connector     | Example                                                                    |
| ------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| If            | If renewable energy becomes affordable, it will replace fossil fuels.      |
| Unless        | Unless governments take immediate action, the damage will be irreversible. |
| Provided that | Provided that funding is available, the project will proceed.              |

---

## 🔹 **10. Drawing Conclusions or Summarizing**

| Basic                          | Advanced Equivalents                 |
| ------------------------------ | ------------------------------------ |
| In conclusion                  | To sum up, All in all, Ultimately    |
| On the whole                   | Generally, Overall, Broadly speaking |
| Therefore / Thus               | Hence, Accordingly, Consequently     |
| Taking everything into account | All things considered, In summary    |

📝 *Example:*

> **To sum up**, stricter environmental laws are vital to ensure a sustainable future.

---

# 🧠 **Band 9 Usage Tips**

✅ **Do:**

* Use **a variety of connectors naturally** (not in every sentence).
* Choose formal, academic connectors (avoid “and”, “but”, “so” repeatedly).
* Place connectors at different positions in sentences for flow.

🚫 **Avoid:**

* Overusing connectors just to sound complex.
* Mixing informal (like “plus”) with formal writing.
* Repeating the same linker too often.

---

# ✍️ **Band 9 Model Example (Task 2 Paragraph)**

> **In recent years**, urbanization has created numerous social and environmental challenges. **For instance**, overcrowding has led to housing shortages and rising costs. **Moreover**, air pollution has worsened due to the increased use of private vehicles. **Therefore**, investing in public transportation and sustainable housing policies is crucial to improve the quality of urban life.
