## 🧭 IELTS Writing Task 1 — Band 9 Vocabulary & Structures

### **Theme: Trends, Charts, and Graphs**

---

## 📊 1. Describing Trends (Change Over Time)

| **Trend Type**    | **Common Verbs**             | **Advanced Alternatives**                                                       | **Example Sentences**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| ----------------- | ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Increase**      | rise, grow, go up            | surge, escalate, shoot up, climb exponentially, witness a rapid upswing         | • Internet access **surged sharply** from 25% to 70% between 2010 and 2020.<br>• The literacy rate **escalated steadily**, reflecting the success of educational reforms.<br>• Solar power adoption **witnessed an exponential rise** after government subsidies were introduced.           |
| **Decrease**      | fall, decline                | plummet, dip, deteriorate, experience a downturn, drop substantially            | • Industrial emissions **plummeted dramatically** following the 2015 environmental act.<br>• The population of rural workers **experienced a gradual downturn** after urban migration began.<br>• Electricity consumption in households **dropped substantially** during the energy crisis. |
| **Stability**     | remain stable, stay the same | plateau, level off, persist unchanged, hover consistently, maintain equilibrium | • The data shows that employment rates **plateaued** after 2018.<br>• For almost a decade, oil demand **hovered consistently** around 50 million barrels per day.<br>• Internet usage **maintained equilibrium**, with negligible annual variation.                                         |
| **Fluctuation**   | vary, change                 | oscillate, vacillate, alternate irregularly, exhibit volatility                 | • The import value **oscillated moderately** between 2012 and 2018.<br>• Rainfall data **exhibited significant volatility**, particularly between June and September.<br>• The share market **vacillated** before stabilizing at a new level.                                               |
| **Peak / Trough** | reach a high/low             | hit a peak, touch the nadir, attain the zenith, culminate                       | • The percentage of mobile users **attained its zenith** in 2019 at 95%.<br>• Employment in the agricultural sector **hit its lowest point** in 2020.<br>• The trend **culminated** in a record-breaking year of production.                                                                |

## 📉 2. Types of Graphs & Representation Vocabulary

| **Type**    | **Common Word**   | **Sophisticated Alternative**                         | **Example Sentences**                                                                                 |
| ----------- | ----------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Line Graph  | line chart        | longitudinal graph, temporal trend chart              | • The **longitudinal graph** delineates population growth over four decades.                          |
| Bar Chart   | bar diagram       | comparative bar graph, quantitative bar matrix        | • The **comparative bar matrix** highlights GDP differences among five nations.                       |
| Pie Chart   | pie chart         | proportional circular diagram                         | • The **proportional circular diagram** illustrates the composition of energy sources in 2020.        |
| Table       | data table        | tabulated dataset, structured data representation     | • The **tabulated dataset** demonstrates revenue growth across different sectors.                     |
| Map         | map illustration  | geographical distribution schematic                   | • The **geographical schematic** depicts the expansion of urban areas between 1990 and 2020.          |
| Process     | flow chart        | sequential process schematic, procedural flow diagram | • The **procedural schematic** explains the stages of water purification.                             |
| Mixed Chart | combination graph | hybrid chart, dual-representation figure              | • The **hybrid chart** combines a line and bar graph to represent both income and expenditure trends. |

## ⚙️ 3. Verbs for Describing Movement

| **Category**         | **Moderate Movement** | **Stronger Movement**                    | **Example Sentences**                                                                                                                   |
| -------------------- | --------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Upward**           | climb, grow, rise     | soar, rocket, shoot up, leap, skyrocket  | • The import of consumer electronics **skyrocketed** in the final quarter.<br>• Urbanization **leapt** from 35% to 70% within a decade. |
| **Downward**         | decline, fall         | tumble, plunge, plummet, slump, collapse | • Coal production **plunged** by 60% due to stricter environmental laws.<br>• Tourism **collapsed** amid travel restrictions.           |
| **Stable / Minimal** | remain, persist       | stagnate, stabilize, consolidate         | • Prices **stagnated** for three consecutive years.                                                                                     |
| **Fluctuating**      | vary, shift           | oscillate, swing unpredictably, waver    | • Currency value **oscillated wildly** throughout the fiscal year.                                                                      |

## ⚡ 4. Rate or Degree of Change

| **Speed / Degree** | **Adverbs**                        | **Adjectives**                        | **Example Sentences**                                                |
| ------------------ | ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Rapid / Strong** | sharply, dramatically, suddenly    | sharp, steep, remarkable, substantial | • Demand **rose sharply**, reflecting increased consumer confidence. |
| **Moderate**       | steadily, progressively, gradually | gradual, consistent, moderate         | • Employment **increased steadily** over the given period.           |
| **Slow / Slight**  | slowly, marginally, mildly         | slight, negligible, modest            | • Export levels **rose slightly**, indicating slow market recovery.  |

## 📊 5. Quantitative and Comparative Language

| **Type**              | **Examples**                                                                                             |
| --------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Proportions**       | roughly one-quarter, nearly two-thirds, a marginal fraction, the overwhelming majority, a small minority |
| **Comparatives**      | higher than, lower in contrast to, approximately double, marginally less, significantly more             |
| **Superlatives**      | the largest share, the smallest percentage, the most significant growth                                  |
| **Range Expressions** | ranging from 20% to 80%, fluctuating between…, peaking at…, averaging around…                            |

🧮 **Example Sentences:**

> • Nearly **two-thirds of Bangladeshi households** had internet access by 2020.
> • Industrial energy use **accounted for the largest share** of total consumption.
> • The production rate **fluctuated between 40 and 60 units** throughout the year.

## 💬 6. Descriptive Overview and Summary Phrases

| **Purpose**              | **Expressions**                                                                                                           |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Overview (beginning)** | Overall, the chart reveals that… / In general, it is evident that… / Broadly speaking, the data demonstrates…             |
| **Comparison (body)**    | In contrast to…, whereas…, while…, on the other hand…, conversely…, unlike…, compared with…                               |
| **Summary (ending)**     | To summarize, it can be observed that… / In essence, the data highlights that… / Generally, the most striking feature is… |

🧮 **Example Sentences:**

> • **Overall**, both industrial and residential sectors **experienced consistent growth**, whereas agricultural demand remained relatively constant.
> • **In contrast to** India, China demonstrated a **faster rate of technological adoption**.
> • **Generally**, the most striking feature is the rapid expansion of renewable energy sources.

## 🔗 7. Advanced Connectors & Linking Devices

| **Function**               | **Connector Phrases (Advanced)**                                                                  |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Addition**               | moreover, furthermore, in addition to this, not only…but also, equally important, correspondingly |
| **Contrast**               | conversely, on the contrary, whereas, while, even though, despite, although, in comparison with   |
| **Cause & Effect**         | consequently, therefore, as a result of, owing to, due to the fact that, thereby, hence           |
| **Sequence / Time**        | initially, subsequently, thereafter, ultimately, over the period of, following this               |
| **Emphasis**               | notably, particularly, significantly, most strikingly, remarkably                                 |
| **Example / Illustration** | for instance, to illustrate, as exemplified by, specifically, namely                              |
| **Conclusion**             | to conclude, in summary, overall, thus, as can be inferred, from this data it is apparent that    |

🧮 **Example Sentence Using Advanced Connectors:**

> **Initially**, the number of internet users was minimal; **however**, it **expanded remarkably** in the following decade. **Consequently**, the digital economy **flourished**, particularly in developing regions.

## 🧠 8. Model Sentences (Band 9 Templates)

1. *The proportion of urban residents **rose steadily**, **reaching a plateau** after 2015.*
2. *The chart **illustrates a marked divergence** between male and female employment trends.*
3. *Sales of electric vehicles **skyrocketed**, **thereby surpassing** traditional car sales in 2020.*
4. *The percentage of renewable energy **doubled over the period**, **culminating in a record high** by 2020.*
5. *While industrial output **expanded consistently**, agricultural production **remained virtually stagnant**.*
6. *It is evident that both variables **demonstrate a positive correlation**, indicating progressive economic improvement.*
