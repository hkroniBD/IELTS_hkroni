# 🧠 IELTS Writing Task 2 — Band 9 Lexical Resource & Structure Masterlist

### (Argument, Opinion, Discussion, Problem-Solution, Advantage-Disadvantage)

---

## 🏗️ 1. High-Band Lexical Categories

| **Purpose**              | **Common Expression**            | **Band 9 Upgrade**                                                                                                           | **Example Sentence**                                                                                                                      |
| ------------------------ | -------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Giving Opinion**       | I think / I believe              | It is widely acknowledged that / It can be argued that / From my perspective / There is substantial evidence to suggest that | *It can be argued that government intervention is essential to regulate industrial pollution in developing countries like Bangladesh.*    |
| **Agree / Disagree**     | I agree / disagree               | I firmly advocate the view that / I am inclined to believe that / This essay strongly contends that                          | *This essay firmly advocates the view that technological literacy should be made a compulsory component of secondary education.*          |
| **Discussing Two Views** | Some people think / others think | While it is often argued that…, others maintain that… / There is an ongoing debate regarding…                                | *There is an ongoing debate regarding whether automation enhances or threatens employment security.*                                      |
| **Presenting Facts**     | It is true that                  | Undeniably / Empirical evidence suggests that / It is an indisputable fact that                                              | *Empirical evidence suggests that excessive screen time deteriorates sleep quality among students.*                                       |
| **Presenting Cause**     | because / since                  | owing to, due to, as a result of, stemming from, triggered by, attributable to                                               | *Urban congestion is largely **attributable to** poor transport planning.*                                                                |
| **Presenting Effect**    | leads to / results in            | gives rise to, causes, culminates in, precipitates, contributes to                                                           | *Deforestation **contributes to** severe soil erosion and loss of biodiversity.*                                                          |
| **Comparing Ideas**      | similar to / different from      | analogous to, in contrast to, conversely, by comparison                                                                      | *Online education is **analogous to** classroom learning in terms of content delivery but **differs significantly** in engagement level.* |

## 🔗 2. Advanced Connectors and Logical Linking Devices

| **Function**                              | **Advanced Connectors (Band 9 Style)**                                                   |
| ----------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Adding Information**                    | furthermore, moreover, in addition, not only…but also, likewise, similarly, coupled with |
| **Contrasting**                           | however, nevertheless, on the contrary, conversely, in contrast, despite this, whereas   |
| **Giving Examples**                       | for instance, for example, to illustrate, namely, specifically, as evidenced by          |
| **Cause and Effect**                      | consequently, therefore, as a result, as a consequence, hence, thus, leading to          |
| **Emphasis**                              | undoubtedly, indeed, notably, above all, it is worth emphasizing that, what is more      |
| **Clarification / Explanation**           | in other words, that is to say, to put it simply, essentially, more precisely            |
| **Sequencing Ideas**                      | firstly, subsequently, thereafter, ultimately, finally, to begin with                    |
| **Concession (acknowledging opposition)** | admittedly, although, even though, while it may be true that, notwithstanding            |
| **Conclusion**                            | to conclude, in summary, overall, on the whole, in essence, all things considered        |

🧮 **Example Paragraph Using Advanced Connectors:**

> **Admittedly**, technology has made communication more efficient. **However**, it has also **precipitated a decline in interpersonal relationships**, particularly among younger generations. **Therefore**, it is crucial to balance virtual and physical interactions to sustain emotional well-being.

## 🧩 3. Categorical Vocabulary by Essay Type

---

### **A. Opinion / Argument Essays**

(*Do you agree or disagree?*)

| **Function**               | **Phrases / Expressions**                                                                             | **Example Sentence**                                                                                                            |
| -------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Stating Strong Opinion** | I strongly believe that / It is my firm conviction that / I am of the opinion that                    | *I strongly believe that renewable energy investment is vital for sustainable development in Bangladesh.*                       |
| **Moderate Opinion**       | I tend to agree / I partially agree / While I agree to some extent                                    | *While I agree that technology has simplified life, it has simultaneously fostered dependency.*                                 |
| **Balancing View**         | Although it is true that…, I maintain that… / While some benefits exist…, the drawbacks outweigh them | *Although it is true that online learning increases accessibility, I maintain that it cannot replace face-to-face interaction.* |
| **Reinforcing Argument**   | This argument is reinforced by…, as evidenced by…, as demonstrated through…                           | *This argument is reinforced by data showing improved literacy rates following digital inclusion programs.*                     |

---

### **B. Discussion Essays**

(*Discuss both views and give your opinion*)

| **Function**            | **Expressions**                                                                                      | **Example Sentence**                                                                               |
| ----------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Introducing View 1**  | One school of thought holds that…, Advocates of this viewpoint argue that…, Some people assert that… | *Advocates of public healthcare argue that it ensures equitable medical access for all citizens.*  |
| **Introducing View 2**  | On the other hand, opponents claim that…, Conversely, critics argue that…                            | *Conversely, critics argue that privatization enhances efficiency and innovation.*                 |
| **Presenting Own View** | In my view, the merits of…far outweigh the drawbacks of…, Having considered both sides…              | *Having considered both sides, it seems that a hybrid approach yields the most effective outcome.* |

---

### **C. Problem–Solution Essays**

| **Function**            | **Expressions**                                                                                              | **Example Sentence**                                                                                        |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Identifying Problem** | One pressing issue is that…, A major concern is…, This has led to…                                           | *One pressing issue in urban areas is inadequate waste management, which leads to environmental pollution.* |
| **Describing Cause**    | This stems from…, This can be attributed to…, The root cause lies in…                                        | *This problem largely **stems from** the lack of recycling infrastructure.*                                 |
| **Presenting Solution** | One viable solution could be…, To address this issue…, A possible remedy involves…                           | *A viable solution could be integrating waste sorting education into school curricula.*                     |
| **Evaluating Solution** | This approach is practical because…, This would be effective provided that…, Although challenging, it could… | *Although challenging, this measure could substantially reduce landfill usage if implemented effectively.*  |

---

### **D. Advantage–Disadvantage Essays**

| **Function**                  | **Expressions**                                                                            | **Example Sentence**                                                                                        |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Introducing Advantages**    | One evident advantage is that…, A key benefit is…, The primary merit of…lies in…           | *One evident advantage of e-commerce is the convenience it offers to consumers.*                            |
| **Introducing Disadvantages** | However, a potential drawback is…, Nevertheless, the downside is…, Despite these benefits… | *Nevertheless, the downside of online shopping includes increased fraud and data privacy risks.*            |
| **Balancing Both**            | While there are obvious merits…, it is equally true that…                                  | *While there are obvious merits to globalization, it is equally true that it threatens cultural diversity.* |

---

### **E. Cause–Effect Essays**

| **Function**                  | **Expressions**                                                       | **Example Sentence**                                                                                                             |
| ----------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Cause**                     | arises from, is caused by, results from, can be traced to             | *The rise in obesity can be traced to sedentary lifestyles and excessive fast-food consumption.*                                 |
| **Effect**                    | results in, gives rise to, leads to, creates, triggers                | *This trend **gives rise to** severe health complications and social costs.*                                                     |
| **Multiple Causes / Effects** | another contributing factor is…, this, combined with…, together they… | *Another contributing factor is the decline in outdoor activities, which, combined with unhealthy diets, worsens the situation.* |

## 🧮 4. Academic Nouns and Nominal Phrases

| **Theme**                | **Band 9 Nouns**                                            |
| ------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Change / Development** | transformation, evolution, advancement, shift, modification |
| **Problem**              | obstacle, issue, predicament, complication, drawback        |
| **Solution / Approach**  | measure, remedy, strategy, intervention, policy, initiative |
| **Effect**               | consequence, repercussion, implication, aftermath           |
| **Advantage**            | benefit, merit, strength, positive outcome                  |
| **Disadvantage**         | limitation, shortcoming, weakness, negative repercussion    |

🧮 **Example Sentence:**

> Government **interventions** and **policy initiatives** can mitigate the **negative repercussions** of unplanned industrialization.

## 🧩 5. Complex Sentence Templates (Band 9 Style)

1. *While some argue that globalization enhances cultural understanding, it simultaneously **erodes indigenous traditions**, leading to cultural homogenization.*
2. *If governments **allocate resources efficiently**, public welfare can be **significantly improved** across developing nations.*
3. *Not only has technology **transformed the job market**, but it has also **redefined interpersonal communication dynamics**.*
4. *Despite the evident drawbacks of automation, it remains **an indispensable catalyst for economic efficiency**.*
5. *There is substantial evidence to suggest that **educational investment correlates directly with long-term national prosperity**.*

## 🏁 8. Elegant Conclusion Starters

| **Expression**             | **Example**                                                                                                                                |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **In conclusion,**         | *In conclusion, while digital technology introduces certain risks, its benefits in improving accessibility and efficiency are undeniable.* |
| **To summarize,**          | *To summarize, a balanced approach between innovation and regulation is crucial for sustainable progress.*                                 |
| **All things considered,** | *All things considered, investment in renewable energy remains the most sustainable path for developing economies.*                        |
| **Ultimately,**            | *Ultimately, the responsibility lies with both individuals and governments to ensure responsible consumption.*                             |
